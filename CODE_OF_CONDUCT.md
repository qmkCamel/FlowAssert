# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the FlowAssert community welcoming, safe and equitable. We respect the dignity, rights and contributions of every person who participates in good faith, regardless of identity, background, experience or status.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, honest and constructive.
- Respect different viewpoints, cultures and levels of experience.
- Give and receive technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality and clearly expressed boundaries.
- Credit sources and keep promotion relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualization or unwanted personal attention.
- Insults, character attacks, deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information, device data, accounts, logs or screenshots without permission.
- Impersonation, evading moderation or knowingly misleading maintainers and contributors.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other spaces where someone officially represents FlowAssert.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Contact the maintainer privately using the contact method published on the [qmkCamel GitHub profile](https://github.com/qmkCamel). If no private contact method is available, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) and include only the minimum information needed.

Maintainers will prioritize safety and confidentiality, investigate in good faith and avoid conflicts of interest. Depending on severity and history, responses may include clarification, a private warning, temporary participation limits, suspension or a permanent ban. Immediate risks may skip lower steps.
