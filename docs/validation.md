## 2026-09-08 — generic device connection instead of a Settings-only entry

- Viewer now uses **Connect device / 连接设备** on Android and iPhone. - Current-screen capabilities do not name a target app and explicitly disable auto-launch, reset, forced launch and termination; Android also skips automatic unlock and retains bounded session idle/action settings. Optional Android Settings resolution no longer removes authorized devices or blocks general connections. Per-device `settingsAvailable=false` disables only that option. Helper/signing checks, platform binding, TTL, replay protection and sole MirrorService ownership remain intact; driver-inferred app IDs cannot confer target-app management authority on an app-less non-launching session.

It identified two necessary protections now covered by regression tests: a platform switch/cancel prevents quick-connect continuation, and a pending explicit Settings connection retains its actual mode/progress when switching away and back. Locale changes and failed checks preserve the selection; other safe controls remain interactive.
